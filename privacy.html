Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Personal Agents is a private tool used only by its owner, on their own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads and labels messages in the owner's Gmail and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar.

Where data goes

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
